About Joseph

Hi, I’m Joseph! I’m a Chicago-based performer with a background in improv and sketch comedy from The Second City and iO Theater. Whether I'm on stage with Blue Text Bubbles or producing Poached Ensembles, I bring high energy and a sharp comedic edge to every project.

When I’m not performing, you can find me diving deep into film history or hanging out with my cats. I’m currently represented by Dream Team Talent.
